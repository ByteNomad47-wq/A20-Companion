A simple offline Android companion for the original Soundcore Sleep A20 (A6611), firmware 01.57.

Download **a20-companion-0.4.7-preview.apk** below. Requires Android 12 or later.

- Earbud/case battery levels and last-update times.
- Bluetooth/Sleep switching, volume and play/pause.
- Find earbuds, battery widgets, Quick Settings tile and app shortcuts.
- Optional automation and experimental charging-record reset.

This edition has its own application ID and signing key. It installs separately from earlier private previews; select your paired earbuds again after installation.

Remove the earbuds from your ears before using Find. Charging repair is experimental and cannot fix a damaged or worn-out battery.

Source, privacy information and usage instructions are in this repository. SHA256SUMS is attached for download verification. No account, internet permission or telemetry.
